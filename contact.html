Frequently Asked Questions

What types of partnerships does QBER offer?

We offer OEM manufacturing, ODM solutions, white-label services, technology licensing, distribution rights, and strategic partnerships tailored to your business needs.

How long does product development typically take?

Standard prototyping takes 4-8 weeks. Rush prototyping is available. Production-ready samples typically require 8-12 weeks depending on complexity and customization level.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ varies by product type and customization. Typically, MOQ ranges from 500 units for textiles to 1,000 units for specialized applications. Flexible terms available for strategic partners.

Are there certifications required for specific markets?

Yes. We guide partners through regional requirements. Common certifications include CE Mark (EU), FDA clearance (USA), and local regulatory approvals. We provide comprehensive documentation support.

How does QBER support marketing and regulatory compliance?

We provide technical documentation, efficacy data, regulatory guidance, compliance templates, and marketing materials. Each partner receives customized support packages.

What is the typical partnership agreement timeline?

Initial consultation to signed agreement typically takes 2-4 weeks. We maintain streamlined processes to move quickly while ensuring both parties' interests are protected.
