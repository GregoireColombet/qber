About QBER

Innovation born from necessity, validated by science

Our Story

QBER was founded by our chairman who faced a personal health crisis. After a severe sports injury resulted in full-body numbness and conventional medicine offered no solutions, he embarked on a transformative journey of research and discovery.

For over a decade, he integrated Nobel Prize-winning photomagnetic resonance principles with Russian biofrequency research, eventually developing the proprietary light-quantum biofrequency embedding technology that powers QBER today. What began as a personal quest for healing has evolved into a transformative technology applicable across healthcare, sports, agriculture, energy, and industrial sectors.

Today, QBER products improve the lives of thousands globally, from professional athletes to farmers to healthcare providers, all without medication, radiation, or invasive procedures. Our commitment to science-backed innovation and measurable results remains our core driving force.

Our Mission

To democratize access to precision bioresonance technology, enabling individuals, athletes, healthcare providers, and enterprises to optimize health, performance, and productivity through safe, non-invasive frequency-based solutions.

We are committed to advancing the frontiers of quantum biology and making quantum frequency resonance technology universally accessible to everyone who can benefit from it.
